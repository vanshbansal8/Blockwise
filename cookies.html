Cookie policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

In short: Blockwise does not set any cookies. It uses your browser's local storage, which is not sent to our server, to remember your progress and settings.

1. Cookies

We do not use first-party or third-party cookies. There are no analytics, advertising or social-media cookies. Our fonts are self-hosted and our data providers are contacted by our server, not your browser.

All of this stays on your device until you delete it. Because it is strictly needed for features you ask for, or is a preference you set, we show a notice rather than ask for permission. We do not use it to track you.

3. Notifications

If you press "Enable notifications" on the Alerts page, your browser asks for permission. You can withdraw it at any time in your browser or phone settings.

4. Managing your data

Open "Privacy settings" in the footer to see what is stored and delete it all with one button. You can also clear site data in your browser settings.

5. If this changes

If we ever add analytics, advertising or other optional tracking, we will show a choice and only switch it on if you agree. See also our privacy policy.
