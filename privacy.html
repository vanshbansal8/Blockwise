Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

In short: Blockwise has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies. Your progress, watchlist, simulator and alerts stay in your own browser. We only receive data if you choose to post a score to the leaderboard.

1. Who we are

Blockwise (blockwise.cc.cd) is a free educational website about Bitcoin and crypto, run by an independent creator ("we", "us"). We decide how and why the data described here is used (the "controller").

Contact: send a direct message on Instagram to @vanshbansal_08.

3. What we do not collect

No email addresses, phone numbers, real names, payment details, precise location, advertising IDs or device fingerprints. We use no analytics, no advertising or social-media tracking scripts, and we do not sell or share personal data for marketing. Please do not put personal information in your nickname.

5. How long we keep data

Browser data stays until you clear it. Leaderboard entries stay until you delete yours, or until we reset the board. Rate-limit counters expire after 60 seconds. Server logs are kept by the hosting provider for its standard period.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Delete everything on this device: open "Privacy settings" in the footer and choose "Delete all data on this device".
  • Remove your leaderboard entry: in "Privacy settings", choose "Remove my score". This also frees your nickname. It works from the device you submitted from.
  • Other rights: depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, to withdraw consent at any time, and to complain to your data protection authority. Contact us using the details above and we will respond within a reasonable time.

7. Children

Blockwise is meant for adults. In India, a person under 18 is a "child" under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before posting anything to the leaderboard: that is the only feature where we receive personal data from you. We do not track children, build profiles, or show them targeted advertising (we show no advertising at all).

8. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Device codes are stored in our database only as a one-way hash. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep personal details out of your nickname.

9. India: your rights under the DPDP Act, 2023

For people in India, we act as the "Data Fiduciary" for the leaderboard data described above and aim to follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its Rules (notified in November 2025; most obligations apply from May 2027). You have the right to:

  • Know what personal data we process and why (this policy, and the "Privacy settings" panel on the site).
  • Correct or erase your data. You can erase your leaderboard entry yourself at any time with "Remove my score".
  • Withdraw consent whenever you like. Withdrawing is as easy as giving it: remove your score, or untick the consent box.
  • Raise a grievance with us, and nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or cannot act for yourself.

Grievance contact: send a direct message to @vanshbansal_08 on Instagram. We aim to reply within 30 days, and always within the time the law allows. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India. Our service providers (Vercel, Upstash) may process data outside India, which the Act permits except to countries the Government restricts.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. If we ever add analytics or advertising, we will ask for your permission first.
