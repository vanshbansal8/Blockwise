Stay safe

Most crypto losses come from scams and mistakes, not from the technology.

Scam spotter

Safety checklist

  • Never share your seed phrase or private key with anyone, ever.
  • Use an authenticator app for 2FA, not SMS.
  • Bookmark official sites; do not click links in DMs or ads.
  • Send a small test transaction before a large one.
  • If the return sounds guaranteed, it is a scam.
  • Only invest money you can afford to lose.
